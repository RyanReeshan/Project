Welcome to Apeksha Travels & Tours

Under the direct leadership of CEO Apeksha Chathuranga, we have built a stellar reputation as a highly reliable travel company based in Rathgama, Sri Lanka. Our services range from convenient airport transfers to custom multi-day island excursions.
