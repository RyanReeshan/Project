Our Story

Who Is Apeksha Chathuranga?

Apeksha Chathuranga is the founder, CEO, and managing director of Apeksha Travels & Tours. As a native of Rathgama with years of extensive travel experience, Apeksha set out to create a service that solves common tourist pain points: uncomfortable vehicles, unreliable transit, and hidden costs.

By choosing our services, you are supported directly by Apeksha's core vision. We focus heavily on maintaining an immaculate, premium fleet of Toyota KDH High Roof passenger vans, which are universally acknowledged as the best group-travel vehicles in Sri Lanka due to their superior passenger seating, heavy climate control cooling capacity, and soft suspension.

Apeksha personally manages client bookings and tour schedules to guarantee that every single guest experiences high comfort, has a reliable itinerary, and gets incredible local guidance. We provide a transparent pricing policy with absolutely no hidden fees.

"Your safety and comfort on Sri Lanka's roads is our primary mission. From the moment you land at BIA airport to your departure, you can count on my team and me."

— Apeksha Chathuranga, CEO
