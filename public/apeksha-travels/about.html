Our Journey

Crafting Memorable Journeys Across Sri Lanka

Welcome to Apeksha Travels & Tours! Established in the heart of Rathgama, Sri Lanka, we are an premier tourist transport and tour operations service. Guided by our founders’ vision of providing highly safe, comfortable, and luxury transport, we have quickly become a trusted travel companion for visitors from all over the globe.

We specialize exclusively in high-roof multi-passenger transport featuring top-of-the-line Toyota HiAce KDH High Roof Vans. Every aspect of our vehicle maintenance, driver screening, and customer support represents the peak of Sri Lankan hospitality.

Our Mission

To provide secure, high-standard, and affordable passenger van solutions and customized tours, assuring comfort and unforgettable memories.

Our Vision

To lead Sri Lanka's luxury passenger van transport industry through continuous service upgrades, reliability, and unparalleled local guiding.

Our Quality Seal Dual Air Conditioning & Safety-First Features on every single tour!
